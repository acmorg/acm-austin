What to expect

Engineers explaining work they actually shipped

ACM Austin is a non-profit chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery, run jointly with IEEE. Architecture, trade-offs, and the thing that broke at 2am — not a vendor deck.

Monthly
◎

Tech talks

Three or four speakers a night, 20 minutes each, with questions that go well past the slides.

Occasional
⌘

Workshops

Laptop open, code running before you leave. Small rooms and direct help from the instructor.

Every session
◈

Community showcase

Members demo what they're building. Most of our speakers gave their first talk right here.
